NINE-BUCKET WEEK: EXERCISE GUIDE
Quick reference for every exercise in the plan. Sets and reps are on the plan page.

GOLDEN RULES
• Fast work first, heavy work second, fatiguing work last
• Sprints and jumps: full rest. Stop when you slow down
• Lifts: leave 1–3 reps in the tank. Never grind to failure
• No sprints, heavy legs or hard conditioning the day before sport

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WARM-UP (before speed days)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Easy jog, 3–4 min
• Leg swings: front-back and side-to-side, 10 each leg
• A-skips: skip forward, driving one knee to hip height, and snap the foot down under your hips. 2 × 20 m
• Build-up runs: 3 × 40 m at 60%, 70% and 80% effort

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SPRINT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
MAX SPRINTS (Mon)
Flat-out runs of 20–40 m.
• Start from a 2-point stance (one foot forward, standing)
• Lean forward, push the ground back hard, and let your stride lengthen as you rise
• Relaxed face and shoulders, arms driving cheek to hip
• Rest 30–60 s per 10 m (30 m sprint = 1.5–3 min rest)
✗ Treating it as cardio. If your rest is short, it's not speed training

ACCELERATION SPRINTS (Thu)
Short 20 m bursts focused on the first steps.
• Use a 3-point start (one hand down) or a falling start (lean until you have to step)
• Stay low and push for the first 5–6 steps, then rise naturally
• Every rep should be crisp. End the session if they get sloppy

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PLYOMETRICS + JUMPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
POGO HOPS (Mon)
Small, fast, two-footed hops in place.
• Legs nearly straight, stiff ankles, bounce off the balls of your feet
• Aim for the shortest possible ground contact. Think "hot floor"
• Height comes from the springy ankle, not from bending the knees
✗ Heels touching down or knees sinking. That turns it into a squat jump

LATERAL / SINGLE-LEG HOPS (Thu)
• Lateral: hop side to side over a line, both feet together, quick and stiff
• Single-leg: hop in place on one leg, same stiff-ankle bounce
• 10 contacts per set, or per leg for single-leg

BROAD JUMPS (Mon + Thu)
Max-effort standing long jump.
• Feet hip-width apart, swing the arms back, quick dip, then explode forward and up
• Land softly in a half squat, feet under you, and stick the landing
• Walk back, reset fully, one rep at a time
• This one correlates with sprint speed, so track your best distance

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SQUAT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SQUAT (Mon): back, front or Zercher, your pick
• Back: bar on your upper traps. Front: bar on the front of your shoulders. Zercher: bar in the crook of your elbows
• Brace your core as if about to take a punch, then sit down between your heels
• Knees track over your toes, chest proud, depth at or below parallel
• Drive up hard through your whole foot
• Target: 1.5× bodyweight. Beyond that, extra squat strength helps speed less
✗ Chasing PBs to failure. It leaves your legs dead for anything fast

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HINGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ROMANIAN DEADLIFT / RDL (Mon)
• Stand tall holding the bar, knees softly bent (and they stay that way)
• Push your hips back like closing a car door with your butt. The bar slides down your thighs
• Lower until you feel a big hamstring stretch, usually just below the knee
• Squeeze your glutes to stand back up. Keep your back flat throughout
• Load it heavy and treat it like a main lift
✗ Bending the knees more as you go down. That turns it into a regular deadlift

BACK EXTENSIONS (Thu)
On a 45° hyperextension bench.
• Hip pad just below your hip bones, so you can bend at the hips
• Lower your torso, then drive your hips into the pad to come up
• For more hamstring focus, round your upper back slightly and push through your hips
• Hold a plate to your chest once 12 reps feel easy

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PULL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PULL-UPS (Tue weighted, Thu bodyweight)
• Any grip: wide, narrow or neutral (palms facing). Rotate between them
• Start from a dead hang, pull your chest toward the bar, chin over
• Lower all the way down under control
• Goal: 15 strict reps. Then add weight with a belt or a dumbbell between your feet
• Advanced: bodyweight + added load = 1.8× bodyweight
✗ Kipping or half reps

CHEST-SUPPORTED ROW (Tue)
Lying face-down on an incline bench, rowing dumbbells.
• Bench at 30–45°, chest on the pad, dumbbells hanging straight down
• Pull your elbows back toward your hips and squeeze your shoulder blades
• Lower slowly to a full stretch
• Your lower back does nothing, so you can push your upper back hard

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
UPPER-BODY POWER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SMITH-MACHINE BENCH THROWS (Tue)
• Light load: 30–40% of your bench
• Lower under control, then press as fast as possible and let the bar leave your hands
• Catch it and go straight into the next rep. That catch-and-release is what builds the spring
• Smith machine only. Never with a free barbell
✗ Going heavy. Speed is the point

PLYO PUSH-UPS (beginner swap for bench throws)
• Push-up position, lower fast, then push explosively so your hands leave the floor
• Land with soft elbows and flow straight into the next rep

PARTNER DROPS (Thu)
• Lie on your back, arms up. A partner drops a medicine ball from standing height
• Catch it, let it sink toward your chest, then throw it straight back up as fast as possible
• No partner? Do banded or plyo push-ups instead

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PUSH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BENCH PRESS (Tue)
• Barbell, dumbbell or incline all count
• Eyes under the bar, shoulder blades squeezed together, feet planted
• Lower to your mid/lower chest, elbows about 45° from your body
• Press up and slightly back toward your face
• Standards: men 1.25× bodyweight, women 0.75× bodyweight (intermediate)

PUSH PRESS (Tue)
• Bar on the front of your shoulders, elbows slightly forward, set up tall
• Dip: bend the knees 10–15 cm (4–6 in), straight down, torso upright, controlled
• Drive: explode up through your legs and punch the bar overhead to lockout
• Check: you should push press 20–30% more than your strict press
✗ A lazy little knee bump. The leg drive is the whole point

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CONDITIONING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ESD INTERVALS (Wed, optional Sun)
• 4 min hard at 80–85% of your max heart rate, then 1 min easy. That's 1 round
• 6–12 rounds depending on level
• Off-feet machines only: air bike, rower or SkiErg. Rotate between them if you can
• Max heart rate estimate: 220 − your age. 80–85% of that is your target zone
• The pace should feel hard but repeatable. You could get out a short sentence

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SKILL / PLAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FRESH DAY (day before sport)
• Rest, or 20–30 min of light skill work: ball handling, footwork, easy mobility
• Nothing that leaves you tired

SPORT / PLAY DAY
• Play your sport hard. It counts as a conditioning session
• No team sport? Join a rec league, play pickup or five-a-side, try climbing or martial arts

Bench ......... 1.25× BW (men) / 0.75× BW (women)
Pull-ups ...... 15 strict reps
Weighted pull . 1.8× BW total (advanced)
Push press .... 20–30% over strict press
