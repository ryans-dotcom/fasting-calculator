NINE-BUCKET WEEK: DAILY WORKOUTS
Sets × reps are Intermediate. (Beginner / Advanced) in brackets.
Plan assumes sport on Saturday. If yours is another day, shift everything so Friday's "Fresh Day" falls the day before.

GOLDEN RULES
• Fast work first, heavy work second, fatiguing work last
• Sprints and jumps: full rest. Stop when you slow down
• Lifts: leave 1–3 reps in the tank. Never grind to failure
• No sprints, heavy legs or hard conditioning the day before sport


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
MONDAY: SPEED + LOWER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Buckets: Sprint, Plyometrics, Squat, Hinge

1. WARM-UP, 10 min
• Easy jog 3–4 min
• Leg swings: front-back and side-to-side, 10 each leg
• A-skips 2 × 20 m: drive one knee to hip height, snap the foot down under you
• Build-up runs 3 × 40 m at 60%, 70%, 80%

2. MAX SPRINTS: 5 × 30 m (Beg 6 × 20 m / Adv 6 × 40 m)
Rest 1.5–3 min (30–60 s per 10 m)
• Start standing, one foot forward
• Lean, push the ground back hard, let your stride open up as you rise
• Relaxed face and shoulders, arms drive cheek to hip
✗ Short rest turns it into cardio. Stop if you're slowing down

3. POGO HOPS: 2 × 10 (Beg 2 × 8 / Adv 2 × 10)
Rest 60 s
• Small fast hops in place, legs nearly straight, stiff ankles
• Shortest possible ground contact: "hot floor"
✗ Heels down or knees sinking

4. BROAD JUMPS: 5 × 1
Rest 60–90 s
• Swing the arms back, quick dip, explode forward and up
• Land softly in a half squat and stick it
• Walk back and fully reset each rep. Track your best distance

5. SQUAT: 4 × 4 (Beg 3 × 6 / Adv 4 × 3)
Back, front or Zercher. Rest 2–3 min
• Brace as if about to take a punch, sit down between your heels
• Knees over toes, chest proud, at or below parallel
• Drive up through your whole foot
✗ Grinding to failure. It kills your speed

6. ROMANIAN DEADLIFT: 3 × 6 (Beg 3 × 8 / Adv 4 × 5)
Rest 2 min
• Soft knees that stay soft. Push your hips back, the bar slides down your thighs
• Lower to a big hamstring stretch (just below the knee), back flat
• Squeeze your glutes to stand. Load it heavy, like a main lift
✗ Bending the knees more as you go down


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TUESDAY: UPPER STRENGTH
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Buckets: Upper power, Push, Pull

1. WARM-UP, 8 min
• Band pull-aparts, scap push-ups, empty-bar bench

2. SMITH-MACHINE BENCH THROWS: 4 × 3 (Adv 5 × 3)
Beginners: PLYO PUSH-UPS 3 × 5 instead. Rest 90 s
• Light load: 30–40% of your bench
• Lower under control, press as fast as possible, and let the bar leave your hands
• Catch it and flow straight into the next rep
• Smith machine only. Never with a free barbell
• Plyo push-ups: lower fast, push so your hands leave the floor, land soft and repeat

3. BENCH PRESS: 4 × 5 (Beg 3 × 8 / Adv 5 × 3)
Rest 2–3 min
• Shoulder blades squeezed, feet planted, eyes under the bar
• Lower to your mid/lower chest, elbows about 45° from your body
• Press up and slightly back
• Target: men 1.25× bodyweight, women 0.75× bodyweight

4. PULL-UPS, WEIGHTED: 4 × 5 (Beg 3 × max bodyweight / Adv 5 × 3)
Rest 2 min
• Any grip, rotate between them. Start from a dead hang, chin over the bar
• Lower all the way down under control
• Stop 1–2 reps short of failure. Add weight once 10 clean reps are easy
✗ Kipping or half reps

5. CHEST-SUPPORTED ROW: 3 × 8 (Beg 3 × 10 / Adv 4 × 8)
Rest 90 s
• Face-down on a 30–45° bench, dumbbells hanging straight down
• Pull your elbows toward your hips, squeeze your shoulder blades
• Lower slowly to a full stretch

6. PUSH PRESS: 3 × 4 (Beg 3 × 6 / Adv 4 × 3)
Rest 2 min
• Bar on the front of your shoulders, stand tall
• Dip: 10–15 cm (4–6 in), straight down, torso upright, controlled
• Drive: explode with your legs and punch the bar to lockout
• Check: you should push press 20–30% more than your strict press
✗ A lazy little knee bump


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WEDNESDAY: CONDITIONING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Bucket: Conditioning

1. WARM-UP: 5 min easy spin or row

2. ESD INTERVALS: 8 rounds (Beg 6 / Adv 10–12)
• 4 min hard at 80–85% of max heart rate, then 1 min easy. That's 1 round
• Air bike, rower or SkiErg. Rotate between them if you can
• Max heart rate estimate: 220 − your age. Aim for 80–85% of that
• Hard but repeatable. You could still say a short sentence

3. COOL-DOWN + MOBILITY: 10 min
• Hips, hamstrings, upper back


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
THURSDAY: SPEED + POWER (LIGHT)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Buckets: Sprint, Plyometrics, Hinge, Upper power, Pull
Keep it snappy. Sport is 2 days away.

1. WARM-UP, 10 min
• Same as Monday: jog, leg swings, A-skips, build-ups

2. ACCELERATION SPRINTS: 6 × 20 m (Adv 8 × 20 m)
Rest 1–2 min
• Start with one hand down, or lean until you have to step
• Stay low and push for the first 5–6 steps, then rise
• Crisp reps only. End it if they get sloppy

3. LATERAL OR SINGLE-LEG HOPS: 1 × 10 (Adv 2 × 10)
Rest 60 s
• Lateral: hop side to side over a line, feet together, quick and stiff
• Single-leg: hop in place on one leg, 10 per leg

4. BROAD JUMPS: 5 × 1
Rest 60–90 s
• Same as Monday: swing, dip, explode, stick the landing, fully reset

5. PARTNER DROPS: 3 × 5 (Adv 3 × 6)
Rest 60 s
• On your back, arms up. A partner drops a med ball from standing height
• Catch it, let it sink toward your chest, throw it straight back up fast
• No partner? Do plyo push-ups or banded push-ups

6. BACK EXTENSIONS: 3 × 12 (Adv 3 × 10 holding a plate)
Rest 60 s
• Hip pad just below your hip bones on a 45° bench
• Round your upper back slightly and drive your hips into the pad to come up
• Hold a plate to your chest once 12 reps feel easy

7. BODYWEIGHT PULL-UPS: 2 × (max − 2)
Rest 90 s
• Strict reps, stopping 2 short of failure
• Relative-strength check. Goal: 15 strict reps


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FRIDAY: FRESH DAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Bucket: Skill
• Rest, or 20–30 min of light skill work: ball handling, footwork, easy mobility
• Nothing that leaves you tired. Tomorrow you play


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SATURDAY: SPORT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Buckets: Skill, Conditioning
• Training or match. Play hard. It counts as a conditioning session
• No team sport? Rec league, pickup, five-a-side, climbing or martial arts, 45–90 min


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SUNDAY: RECOVERY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Rest, sleep, eat, light walk
• Optional if you feel fresh: ESD 4–6 rounds (Beg 4 / Adv 6), off-feet only


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4-WEEK BLOCK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Week 1: as written
Week 2: a little more load on lifts, +1 ESD round
Week 3: heaviest week, top sets hard but no grinding
Week 4: deload. About 40% fewer sets, keep sprints short and fast, retest a standard
